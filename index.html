The Problem

Three Failures.
One System Can Fix Them.

Urban India sits at the intersection of alarming food waste and persistent food insecurity — both happening in the same building, every day.

01

The Forgotten Fridge Problem

Deep Indian refrigerators become black holes for perishables. Groceries from Blinkit and Zepto pile up, expire unseen, and are thrown away — ₹40,000+ per household annually.

02

The Surplus With No Home

Indian households routinely cook 20–30% more than needed. Surplus daal, sabzi, and rice either spoils or finds no dignified channel to someone who needs it.

03

Workers in the Same Building Go Hungry

Security guards, housekeeping staff, and drivers live within 50 metres of this surplus every day. There is no trusted, structured system to bridge that gap.

Questions We

We've pressure-tested every assumption. Here are honest answers to the sharpest questions.

"The potential for ESG-linked brand sponsorships on a captive premium audience is genuinely differentiated. The unit economics at society level are compelling."

— Seed Investor Perspective

How do you ensure food safety and liability?
▼
Every donation is logged via QR with cooking time and ingredients. The platform enforces a 4-hour window for cooked food in ambient lockers and 24 hours in cold slots. Donors accept community T&Cs. We follow FSSAI guidelines for food redistribution and partner with FSSAI-registered NGOs for extended distribution.
Why would societies pay for this?
▼
Premium housing societies in India actively compete on sustainability credentials. A Sanji-certified society gets an ESG rating badge, reduces collective waste by a measurable percentage, and provides a tangible welfare benefit for their employed staff — three strong motivators for RWA committees.
What prevents someone building a copy?
▼
Defensibility comes from hardware (the Hub locker system has 14-month manufacturing lead times), network effects within societies (a building with 200 active users is far stickier than zero), proprietary waste-pattern data that improves with scale, and deep RWA relationships that create high switching costs.
What does the $2.5M seed fund specifically?
▼
40% goes to manufacturing 150 Smart Hubs for our pilot 50-society GTM. 35% funds tech (app development, OCR pipeline, NFC infrastructure). 20% covers GTM — RWA partnerships, FMCG brand sales, and community manager hires. 5% is reserved for working capital and legal.
What's the path to Series A?
▼
18-month milestone: 500 onboarded societies across 4 cities, ₹2.5Cr ARR from society SLAs, 2 signed FMCG brand sponsorship deals, and a waste-reduction dataset covering 50,000+ households. These metrics unlock a Series A targeting pan-India expansion and Southeast Asia entry.
